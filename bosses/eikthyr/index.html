Eikthyr: location, summoning and tips

Eikthyr, the lightning stag, is the first boss in Valheim. He waits in the Meadows and is a gentle test of the basics: blocking, dodging and managing stamina.

How to find Eikthyr

Eikthyr's altar always stands in the Meadows and usually only a few hundred metres from the spawn point. The glowing red runestone at the sacrificial stones marks the altar on your map as soon as you read it.

To summon him, place 2 Deer Trophies on the altar. Deer drop a trophy roughly every second kill. Sneak up on them against the wind or hunt them with a crude bow.

Preparation

  • Craft a full set of leather armour and upgrade it once if you can.
  • Take a flint spear, flint axe or club, a crude bow with about 50 arrows and a wooden shield.
  • Eat three different foods before the fight, for example cooked boar meat, cooked neck tail and raspberries or mushrooms.
  • Sleep first so you fight with the Rested bonus. It noticeably speeds up stamina regeneration.

Attacks

  • Antler ram: he lunges and swings his antlers. Block it with your shield or step to the side.
  • Lightning bolt: he rears up and fires a line of lightning straight ahead, mostly when nobody stands next to him. Dodge sideways.
  • Lightning stomp: he rises on his hind legs and slams down, hitting everything around him. Roll backwards when he rears up.

Tips and tricks

  • Keep the round shield raised and attack right after each of his attacks. Almost everything he does can be blocked, even with a simple wooden shield.
  • Never let your stamina drop to zero. Without stamina you can neither block nor dodge, and that is how most first attempts fail.
  • The rocks and trees around the altar stop his lightning. Use them as cover if you prefer the bow.
  • Fight during the day. At night Greydwarves and other creatures join in.
  • If the fight goes badly, run. Eikthyr does not disappear, and he keeps the damage you dealt for a while.

Reward

Eikthyr drops 3 Hard Antlers and his trophy. The antlers make the Antler Pickaxe, your first pickaxe, which lets you mine tin and copper in the Black Forest and start the Bronze Age.

His Forsaken power makes running and jumping much cheaper. It stays useful for the whole game, for exploring, fleeing and carrying ore.
