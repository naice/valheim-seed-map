The Elder: location, summoning and tips

The Elder is a giant living tree and the second boss. He rules the Black Forest and punishes players who fight him in the open. With fire arrows and a pillar to hide behind, he is very manageable.

How to find The Elder

The Elder's altar stands in the Black Forest: a stone platform with four tall pillars around a fire bowl. Red runestones in the Burial Chambers and in some ruined stone towers mark the nearest altar on your map.

To summon him, burn 3 Ancient Seeds in the fire bowl. Seeds drop from Greydwarf Brutes and Shamans and always from destroyed Greydwarf nests, the purple glowing spawners.

Preparation

  • Bronze armour is ideal. Troll leather armour is a good alternative and much cheaper.
  • Bring a finewood bow and at least 100 fire arrows. The Elder is weak to fire and the burning effect keeps ticking.
  • Take a bronze buckler or banded shield and an axe for the roots.
  • Brew a few minor healing meads and eat your best three foods, for example deer stew, carrot soup and queens jam.
  • Clear Greydwarf nests around the altar, cut down nearby trees and build a small shelter with a portal a short distance away.

Attacks

  • Vine shot: he stretches out his arms and fires a volley of vines over a long distance. Stand behind one of the four pillars. They cannot be destroyed.
  • Stomp: up close he stomps the ground for heavy damage. Keep your distance or roll away.
  • Roots: he raises a field of roots around you that whip anyone nearby. Walk out of the area. The roots disappear after a while, or you can cut them down with an axe.

Tips and tricks

  • Play around the pillars: step out, shoot two or three fire arrows, step back before the vines arrive. This simple rhythm wins the fight.
  • Do not fight in melee unless you have to. The stomp hits much harder than anything else at this point of the game.
  • Falling trees are a real danger. The Elder knocks them down with his vines, and a falling trunk can kill you outright. Clearing the arena beforehand removes that risk.
  • Summon him in the morning. The fight can take several minutes, and at night the forest gets crowded.
  • A campfire or bonfire near the arena keeps you Rested and its fire damages him if he walks through it.

Reward

The Elder drops the Swamp Key and his trophy. The key opens the Sunken Crypts in the Swamp, the only good source of iron.

After the Elder is defeated, Haldor adds Ymir Flesh and Thunder Stones to his stock, and the Bog Witch sells more ingredients.

His Forsaken power speeds up woodcutting and mining considerably. It is perfect for big building projects and mining trips.
