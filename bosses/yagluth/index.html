Yagluth: location, summoning and tips

Yagluth, the fallen king of the Fulings, is the fifth boss. Only his upper body crawls across the Plains, but his fire and meteors make him one of the deadliest fights in the game. Fire resistance is not optional here.

How to find Yagluth

Yagluth's altar stands in the Plains: a ring of huge stone fingers reaching out of the ground. Red runestones at stone circles in the Plains mark it on your map. They are rare, so the map saves you a long search here.

To summon him, place 5 Fuling Totems on the altar. You find one totem in most Fuling villages, and Fuling Berserkers sometimes drop one. Totems can be taken through portals.

Preparation

  • Brew fire resistance barley wine and take several per player. It lasts 10 minutes and has to be active for the entire fight.
  • Wear fully upgraded padded armour. Wolf armour is the minimum.
  • Bring a blackmetal sword, the Porcupine or Frostner. Yagluth strongly resists pierce damage, so spears, atgeirs and most arrows are poor choices.
  • If you want to fight at range, use frost arrows and expect a long fight.
  • Take medium healing and stamina meads, and eat the best Plains food: lox meat pie, blood pudding, fish wraps.
  • Clear all Fulings, lox and deathsquitos around the altar first and place a portal at a safe distance.

Attacks

  • Fire breath: he raises his head and sweeps a long beam of fire after you. Run sideways or step behind one of the stone fingers.
  • Fist slam: his fist glows blue, then he punches the ground and causes a large explosion. Move away as soon as you see the glow.
  • Meteors: his hand glows orange and meteors rain on your position for several seconds. Keep running in one direction until it stops.

Tips and tricks

  • Every attack is announced by a clear glow. Learn the three signals and the fight becomes calm: attack, see the glow, move, repeat.
  • Attack from behind or from the side and only two or three hits at a time. Greed is what kills players here.
  • The stone fingers block his fire breath, but meteors go over them. Do not rest behind a pillar during the meteor shower.
  • Burning makes you panic and waste stamina. With barley wine active, fire damage is roughly halved and the fight is forgiving.
  • Activate Bonemass's power when you start the melee phase. Renew the barley wine early, not when it runs out.
  • Yagluth is slow. If you need to heal or eat, simply walk away from him.

Reward

Yagluth drops 3 Torn Spirits and his trophy. A Torn Spirit builds the Wisp Fountain. It collects wisps, which you need for the Wisplight that clears the mist in the Mistlands.

After his defeat Haldor sells eggs for raising chickens, and the Bog Witch adds the spice of the Plains.

His Forsaken power raises all your damage by a tenth. It is a strong choice for the Mistlands and later bosses.
