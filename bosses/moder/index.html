Moder: location, summoning and tips

Moder is the dragon mother of the Mountains and the fourth boss. She attacks from the air and on the ground, and her frost breath freezes anyone standing in it. A good bow and frost protection decide this fight.

How to find Moder

Moder's altar lies on a mountain top, usually on a wide, flat plateau. Red runestones in stone ruins in the Mountains and in Frost Caves mark it on your map.

To summon her, place 3 Dragon Eggs on the altar. The eggs lie in nests in the Mountains. Each one weighs 200 and cannot be taken through a portal, so you carry them one at a time, or with three players in a single trip.

Preparation

  • Wear wolf armour: the chest piece and the cape protect you from freezing. Without it you need frost resistance mead for the whole trip.
  • Bring the best bow you have, ideally Draugr Fang, and 150 or more arrows. Moder is weak to fire, so fire arrows are cheap and strong. Obsidian and poison arrows are good too. Frost arrows do nothing.
  • Take a silver sword or another upgraded melee weapon for the phases on the ground.
  • Pack medium healing meads and stamina meads, and eat food with a lot of health, such as serpent stew, sausages and turnip stew.
  • Clear the plateau of wolves, drakes and golems before you start, and set up a portal with a small shelter below the summit.

Attacks

  • Ice volley (in the air): she flies over you and shoots a line of ice projectiles that explode into crystals. Keep running sideways.
  • Frost breath (on the ground): a wide cone of frost straight ahead that slows you massively. Move to her side as soon as she lowers her head.
  • Bite and claws: at close range she bites and swipes. Both can be blocked with a good shield.

Tips and tricks

  • Shoot her whenever she is in the air or landing. Her flight path is slow and predictable, so most arrows hit.
  • When she lands, circle to her flank. The breath only hits what is in front of her, and from the side you can attack freely.
  • Being hit by frost makes you slow and easy to hit again. If you are frozen, block instead of running.
  • The ice crystals from her volley can be destroyed, and they are a small source of obsidian-free cover in the open.
  • Bonemass's power makes the melee phases much safer. With the bow, Eikthyr's power helps you keep moving.
  • Carry the eggs up before the fight and leave them at the altar. They do not disappear.

Reward

Moder drops 10 Dragon Tears and her trophy. The tears build the Artisan Table, which unlocks the windmill, blast furnace and spinning wheel, and with them the gear of the Plains.

After her defeat Haldor sells Wider Pockets, an extra inventory row, and the Bog Witch adds several items to her stock.

Her Forsaken power gives you a tailwind on every voyage and a lot of extra carry weight. It is the favourite of every sailor and ore hauler.
