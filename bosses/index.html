Valheim bosses in order

Valheim's story is told through its bosses, the Forsaken. Each one guards a biome, and each one drops the item that opens the next step: the antler pickaxe for mining, the key to the swamp crypts, the wishbone that finds silver. Beating them in order is the backbone of every playthrough.

The table shows all bosses of the main progression with their biome, health and the items you have to sacrifice. Each boss has its own page with weaknesses, attack patterns and practical tips. Every world has several altars per boss. Enter your seed on the map to see where yours are.

Every defeated boss leaves a trophy. Hang it on its hook at the sacrificial stones to unlock the boss's Forsaken power. You can have one power selected at a time. It lasts 5 minutes and then needs 20 minutes to recharge.

In multiplayer, bosses get tougher and hit harder for every additional player nearby, so more players do not make a fight trivial.
