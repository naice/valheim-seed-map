Bonemass: location, summoning and tips

Bonemass is a huge blob of bones and slime in the Swamp and the third boss. Many players fail here at first because swords and arrows barely scratch him. With a mace and poison resistance, the fight turns around completely.

How to find Bonemass

Bonemass's altar is a giant skull lying in the Swamp. Red runestones inside Sunken Crypts mark the nearest one on your map.

To summon him, drop 10 Withered Bones into the skull. You find them in the Sunken Crypts, in chests and in the muddy scrap piles you dig through for iron anyway.

Preparation

  • Bring an iron mace, upgraded as far as you can. Bonemass is weak to blunt damage and resists slash, pierce and fire. The Stagbreaker or an iron sledge work as well.
  • Brew poison resistance mead and take at least three or four per player. Without it his poison cloud kills in seconds.
  • Wear upgraded iron armour or the root set, and carry an iron-banded shield or better.
  • Take medium healing meads and stamina meads, and eat sausages, turnip stew and similar swamp-tier food.
  • Level the ground around the skull with a hoe so you do not fight knee-deep in water, where leeches bite and stamina does not regenerate while swimming.
  • Build a small roofed shelter with a bed, fire and portal nearby, ideally on raised ground.

Attacks

  • Punch: a wide swing with more reach than it seems, dealing blunt and poison damage. Block or parry it with a solid shield.
  • Poison cloud: he leans back and spews a large cloud that lingers. Run out of it at once and wait until it fades.
  • Summon: he rips off a lump of himself and throws it. Skeletons and blobs spawn where it lands. Kill them quickly before they surround you.

Tips and tricks

  • Drink poison resistance mead before you summon him and renew it when the icon starts to blink. It lasts 10 minutes.
  • The summoning animation is your best opening: he stands still for several seconds while he throws.
  • Parrying his punch staggers nothing, but it protects you fully. Hit two or three times afterwards, then back off.
  • Frost arrows are the only arrows worth using on him. If you already have obsidian and freeze glands, they make a safe ranged fight possible.
  • Use Eikthyr's power to get out of the poison cloud quickly, or the Elder's for the extra regeneration.
  • The Stagbreaker's area attack hits Bonemass and the summoned adds at the same time.

Reward

Bonemass drops the Wishbone and his trophy. With the Wishbone equipped, you detect hidden silver veins in the Mountains, buried treasure in the Meadows and muddy scrap piles in the Swamp.

His Forsaken power is the best defensive power in the game. Many players keep it for every later boss fight.
