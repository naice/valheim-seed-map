The Queen: location, summoning and tips

The Queen is the mother of the Seekers and the sixth boss. She lives deep inside the Infested Citadel in the Mistlands and never fights alone: her brood hatches around you all the time. Good preparation and control of her spawn decide the fight.

How to find The Queen

The Queen is not summoned. She waits inside the Infested Citadel, a huge sealed building in the Mistlands. Red runestones in the Infested Mines mark the citadel on your map.

The door only opens with the Sealbreaker. You craft it at the Galdr Table from 9 Sealbreaker Fragments, which you find in the Infested Mines. To fight her again later, place 3 Seeker Soldier Trophies on the altar inside.

Preparation

  • Wear upgraded carapace armour for melee, or the eitr-weave robe if you fight with magic.
  • She resists pierce damage. Use slash or blunt weapons such as Mistwalker, Krom or the Demolisher. Bows and spears do little.
  • Magic is very strong here: the Staff of Embers clears her brood, the Staff of Protection shields the group and the Dead Raiser distracts enemies.
  • Drink poison resistance mead before you enter, and take major healing meads, stamina meads and eitr meads.
  • Eat the best Mistlands food. For magic users, at least one eitr food is mandatory.
  • The citadel is free of mist, but bring your Wisplight for the way there. A Feather Cape lets you jump down from the upper floors safely.
  • You can build inside the citadel. A workbench, a portal just outside the door and a few stone walls for cover help a lot.

Attacks

  • Slash and rush: wide swings with her front legs and a crawling charge. Dodge sideways or block with a strong shield.
  • Bite: a lunge straight ahead with heavy pierce and poison damage. Do not stand in front of her head.
  • Ground stab: she rears up and stabs the ground with all four arms, hitting everything around her. Back off when she rises.
  • Spit: several poison projectiles that explode and release Seeker Broods.
  • Call: she screams and summons Seekers and broods.
  • Burrow: she digs into the ground and reappears somewhere else in the hall.

Tips and tricks

  • Kill the brood constantly. A dozen small Seekers do more damage than the Queen herself. Area attacks like the Demolisher or the Staff of Embers are ideal.
  • Use the stairs and upper floors of the citadel. She is fast in the open but slow on the ramps, which gives you time to heal and regain stamina.
  • All her melee attacks throw you back. Keep your back away from walls and ledges.
  • In a group, one player with a tower shield holds her attention while the others deal with the adds and attack from behind.
  • Bonemass's power is excellent for melee. Magic users take her own power into the rematch, or Yagluth's for more damage.
  • If you die, your tombstone lies inside the hall. A portal right outside the door makes the corpse run short.

Reward

The Queen drops 5 Majestic Carapaces and her trophy. The carapace builds the Artisan Press, which you need for the gear and the ships that take you to the Ashlands.

After her defeat Haldor sells Deeper Pockets, another inventory row, and the Bog Witch adds the spice of the Mistlands.

Her Forsaken power doubles eitr regeneration. For magic builds it is the best power in the game.
