Haldor: location, wares and prices

Haldor is a dwarven trader who camps in the Black Forest with his lox Halstein. He sells some of the most useful items in the game, above all the belt Megingjord, which lets you carry 150 more weight.

Show Haldor on the map

Where to find Haldor

Haldor's camp is always in the Black Forest. The game prepares up to ten possible camps per world, usually more than 1,500 metres from the centre of the map. The first one you get close to becomes the real camp, and all the others disappear.

When you come within a few hundred metres, a bag icon appears on your map. The camp is surrounded by a protective bubble that keeps enemies out. Building is not possible inside it, but a portal right outside the bubble works well.

Tips

  • Buy Megingjord first. 150 extra carry weight helps with everything from mining to sailing home with iron.
  • The Dverger Circlet is a headlamp that leaves your hands free. It is very handy in crypts and caves, but it takes up the helmet slot.
  • Wider Pockets and Deeper Pockets each add a permanent extra row to your inventory. They are expensive but worth every coin.
  • Ymir Flesh is needed for the Iron Sledge, the Frostner and other strong items. Thunder Stones are needed for the Obliterator.
  • An Egg hatches into a chicken if you keep it warm. Two hens are enough to breed your own supply of eggs and chicken meat, so you only need to buy two.
  • The Fishing Rod only works with bait. The simple bait Haldor sells is the base for every special bait in the game.
  • Save up before the trip: with about 2,000 coins you can afford the belt, the circlet and the fishing rod in one visit.

What you can sell

Coins are the only currency, and you get them by selling valuables. All three traders buy the same items at the same prices. Coins, rubies and amber are found in Burial Chambers, Troll Caves, Sunken Crypts and Fuling villages.
