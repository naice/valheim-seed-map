Hildir: location, wares and prices

Hildir is Haldor's sister. She sells clothes, hats and festive items in the Meadows. Most of her stock was stolen, and she asks you to bring back three chests from three special dungeons.

Show Hildir on the map

Where to find Hildir

Hildir's camp lies in the Meadows, but far from the spawn point, usually at least 3,000 metres from the centre of the map. As with Haldor, there are up to ten possible camps, and the first one you approach becomes the real one.

A shirt icon appears on your map when you get close. At her camp stands a map table. Reading it marks the nearest of her quest dungeons on your map.

What Hildir sells

At first she only sells a handful of items. Each chest you return unlocks another part of her stock.

Tips

  • The Brass Chest lies in a Smouldering Tomb in the Black Forest, the Silver Chest in a Howling Cavern in the Mountains and the Bronze Chest in a Sealed Tower in the Plains. Each one is guarded by a mini boss.
  • The chests cannot be taken through portals and they are heavy. Plan the way back on foot, by cart or by ship before you go in.
  • The quest dungeons get harder in this order. Brenna in the tomb deals fire damage, Geirrhafa in the cavern deals frost, and the two Fuling brothers Zil and Thungr in the tower fight together.
  • All quest dungeons are on the map as well. Switch on the Hildir quest markers to find the ones closest to her camp.
  • The Barber Kit builds a barber station, where you can change your hair and beard at any time.
  • Her clothes are mostly for looks, but they are light and do not slow you down. The Harvest outfits are a favourite for farming.
  • The Iron Pit is a decorative fire pit, and fireworks need a bonfire to launch.

What you can sell

Coins are the only currency, and you get them by selling valuables. All three traders buy the same items at the same prices. Coins, rubies and amber are found in Burial Chambers, Troll Caves, Sunken Crypts and Fuling villages.
