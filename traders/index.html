Valheim traders

Three traders live in Valheim, and each of them is hidden somewhere different in every world. Haldor sells useful gear in the Black Forest, Hildir sells clothes in the Meadows and sends you on a quest, and the Bog Witch sells ingredients for cooking and brewing in the Swamp.

Searching for them without help can take dozens of hours. Enter your seed on the map and you will see every possible camp at once. All prices on these pages are taken directly from the game data.

HaldorBlack Forest · gear, fishing and more inventory space HildirMeadows · clothing, hairdresser and a three-part quest The Bog WitchSwamp · ingredients for feasts, meads and farming

What you can sell

Coins are the only currency, and you get them by selling valuables. All three traders buy the same items at the same prices. Coins, rubies and amber are found in Burial Chambers, Troll Caves, Sunken Crypts and Fuling villages.
