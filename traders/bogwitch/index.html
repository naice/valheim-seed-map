The Bog Witch: location, wares and prices

The Bog Witch lives in a crooked hut in the Swamp with her giant Kvastur. She sells ingredients that you cannot get anywhere else: spices for the big feasts, items for new meads and useful things for your farm.

Show The Bog Witch on the map

Where to find The Bog Witch

The Bog Witch's camp is in the Swamp. Here, too, the game prepares up to ten possible camps, and the first one you get close to stays in your world.

A cauldron icon appears on your map when you are near. Her camp is protected against enemies, which makes it a welcome safe spot in the Swamp. Still, bring poison resistance mead for the way there.

Tips

  • The spices are the key ingredient of the feasts. A feast placed on a table feeds the whole group with one of the strongest foods of its tier, and it lasts for many servings.
  • The Serving Tray lets you carry a prepared feast and set it down somewhere else, for example right before a boss fight.
  • The Scythe Handle is needed for the scythe, which harvests whole fields in one swing. It is one of the best purchases for anyone who farms a lot.
  • Candle Wicks are needed for candles, and Ivy Seeds grow climbing ivy on your buildings.
  • Fresh Seaweed, Cured Squirrel Hamstring, Powdered Dragon Eggshells and Pungent Pebbles are ingredients for the newer meads, which improve skills such as swimming and running or protect against lightning.
  • Toadstools and Fragrant Bundles become available after Moder. They are used for the berserkir mead and for a mead that keeps you clean of negative effects.
  • Her wares are cheap compared with Haldor's. A few hundred coins are enough for a first visit.

What you can sell

Coins are the only currency, and you get them by selling valuables. All three traders buy the same items at the same prices. Coins, rubies and amber are found in Burial Chambers, Troll Caves, Sunken Crypts and Fuling villages.
