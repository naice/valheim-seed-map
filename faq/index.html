Valheim-Map: your whole world from the seed

Valheim-Map is a free interactive map for Valheim. Type in the seed of your world and the map shows the complete world without fog: every biome, the coastline and the exact position of bosses, traders, dungeons and more than 90 other location types.

Nothing is uploaded and no save file is needed. The map runs the same world generation as the game directly in your browser, so the positions match your world down to the metre. Click a marker to get its coordinates and a ready-made console command to travel there.

Use the list on the left to switch location types on and off, search for a specific place such as Haldor or the Sunken Crypts, and save seeds you want to come back to. The link in the address bar always contains the seed and the current view, so you can share any spot with your friends.

How to find your world seed

Every Valheim world is created from a seed, a short text of up to ten characters. The same seed always produces the same world.

  1. Start Valheim and click Start game, then pick your character.
  2. In the world list, select your world. The seed is shown right next to the world name.
  3. On a dedicated server, the seed is stored in the world's .fwl file. Load the world once in single player or ask the server admin for it.
  4. Type the seed into the field at the top left of the map and press Load. Upper and lower case matter.

Frequently asked questions

Is the map accurate for my world?

Yes. The terrain and all locations are calculated with the same algorithm the game uses, and the results were checked against real worlds. The map shows where the game places a location when you first explore the area. Things that players change later, such as destroyed buildings, are not visible.

Which game version does the map support?

The map follows the current public version of Valheim, including the Ashlands and the Deep North. Worlds that were created with a much older version can differ in areas that were explored before an update.

Why are there several markers for Haldor, Hildir and the Bog Witch?

The game prepares up to ten possible camps for each trader. The first camp you get close to becomes the real one, and the others disappear from your world. So you can simply pick the marker that is closest to your base.

Do I have to upload my save file?

No. The seed is all the map needs. The calculation happens on your device, and nothing about your world is sent to a server.

How do I travel to a location I found on the map?

Click the marker. The popup shows the coordinates and a console command. In single player you can enable the console with the launch option -console, type devcommands and then use the goto command. Without cheats, use the coordinates and the distance from the spawn point to navigate.

Does the map work on a phone?

Yes. The map works in every modern browser, including phones and tablets. Calculating all locations takes a few seconds, on older devices a bit longer.

Is Valheim-Map free?

Yes, completely, and without an account. If the map saved you a few hours of sailing, you can support the project with a small donation.
