The best Valheim seeds

A good seed saves hours: a trader within walking distance, the first bosses on your starting continent, or a swamp full of crypts just across the river. The problem with most seed lists is that they are copied from each other and were never checked after world generation changed.

This list is different. We collected the most recommended seeds from the community and recalculated every single one with the world generator of the current game version. The distances below are measured from the spawn point, and every seed opens on the interactive map with one click. Where an old recommendation no longer holds, we say so or left the seed out.

1 The best all-rounder

13o00t Open on the map

If you only try one seed, take this one. The first five bosses all lie within 3.5 km of the spawn point: Eikthyr is 120 m away, the Elder 1.1 km, Moder under 1 km, Bonemass 1.9 km and Yagluth 3.4 km. Hardly any other world packs its progression this tightly.

Haldor camps 1.6 km from the sacrificial stones, close to the minimum distance the game allows. That makes the seed ideal for a quick playthrough and for groups that want to fight bosses instead of sailing for hours.

1fp5ex Open on the map

How to use a seed

In Valheim, click Start game, choose your character and then New in the world list. Enter a world name and type the seed exactly as shown here, including upper and lower case. On a dedicated server, create the world locally first and upload the world files, or set the seed through your host's settings.

The distances are straight lines from the sacrificial stones. A trader counts only once: the game prepares several possible camps, and the first one you approach becomes the real one. The table always uses the nearest one.
